Privacy Policy

Influmart · Last updated: 21 July 2026

Influmart ("we", "us", "our") operates the Influmart platform and website at https://www.influmart.in and the Influmart mobile applications (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Service, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Influmart is a marketplace that connects brands with social-media influencers. Creators can verify their social accounts and share performance analytics; brands can discover creators, view verified statistics, and communicate to arrange collaborations.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account details: name, username, email address, phone number, password (stored encrypted/hashed).
  • Profile details: category, bio, hashtags, pricing, location, and profile photo.
  • Social handles you enter (Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, etc.).
  • Messages you send to other users on the platform.
  • Payment-related information processed by our payment provider (Razorpay). We do not store full card details.

Information from connected social accounts (with your consent)

When you connect or verify a social account, we access—only with your authorization—publicly available and account-owner analytics such as:

  • Follower/subscriber counts, engagement metrics, and audience demographics.
  • Post/video performance (views, likes, comments, shares) and recent content metadata.
  • For YouTube: your channel information and analytics via Google APIs (see Section 6).

Information collected automatically

  • Basic usage and device/log data needed to operate and secure the Service.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and profile.
  • To verify social accounts and display verified analytics to relevant brands.
  • To generate content insights and recommendations for creators.
  • To enable discovery, messaging, and collaborations between brands and creators.
  • To process subscriptions/payments and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To communicate service-related notices (e.g., verification codes, updates).

4. How we share information

  • With other users: your public profile and verified stats are visible to brands on the platform; messages are shared with the recipient.
  • With service providers who help us operate the Service, under confidentiality obligations — including Google (authentication & YouTube data), Meta/Instagram & Facebook (account data), RapidAPI providers (social analytics), Razorpay (payments), Resend (transactional email), and Google Gemini / Google AI (to generate content insights from your post captions/metrics).
  • For legal reasons if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. AI-generated insights

To provide "Content Insights", limited post data (such as captions, engagement figures, and content type) may be sent to a third-party AI provider (Google Gemini) to summarize what content performs well. This data is used only to generate your insights and is handled according to the provider's terms.

6. Google API Services & YouTube data

Influmart's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Our use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • What we access: with your authorization, your YouTube channel details and YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, subscribers, likes, comments, shares) for your own channel.
  • How we use it: to verify channel ownership and display your performance analytics and insights on your Influmart profile.
  • Storage: we store the resulting metrics and an OAuth refresh token to periodically update your analytics. We do not use this data for advertising.
  • Revoking access: you can revoke Influmart's access to your Google account at any time via https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Data retention & deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account from within the app (Account Settings → Delete account) or by emailing us, after which we delete or anonymize your personal data except where retention is required by law.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to safeguard your data.

9. Children

The Service is not intended for individuals under 18 (or the age of majority in your region).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
