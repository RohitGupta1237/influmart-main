Child Safety Standards

Influmart · Last updated: 25 July 2026

Influmart ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting children and maintaining a platform free of child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). We have zero tolerance for such content or conduct. This page describes our standards in line with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

1. Adults-only service

Influmart is a professional marketplace connecting brands with social-media influencers. Our Terms of Service require all users to be at least 18 years old. The Service is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to create an account.

2. Prohibited conduct

The following are strictly prohibited on Influmart:

  • Any child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or content that sexualises minors.
  • Grooming, solicitation, sextortion, trafficking, or any exploitation of minors.
  • Sharing, requesting, or linking to CSAE material of any kind.

3. Preventing and addressing abuse

  • We remove violating content and accounts as soon as we become aware of them.
  • Users can report abuse or CSAE concerns to us at the contact below; reports are reviewed promptly.
  • We take action against offending accounts, including immediate suspension or termination.

4. Reporting to authorities

Where we identify apparent CSAM or CSAE, we act in accordance with applicable law, including reporting to relevant authorities and, where appropriate, to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the relevant local authority.

5. Child safety point of contact

To report a child-safety concern or to reach our child-safety contact, email rohitgupta12371380@gmail.com. We aim to acknowledge and act on reports without undue delay.

6. Compliance

We comply with applicable child-safety laws and with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy. We review these standards regularly and update them as needed.
