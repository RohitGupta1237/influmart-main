Delete Your Account

Influmart · Last updated: 25 July 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Influmart account and the data associated with it. Influmart is operated by Influmart (https://www.influmart.in).

Option 1 — Delete from within the app

  1. Open the Influmart app (or influmart.in) and log in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account, then confirm by tapping "I'm sure, delete my account".

Your account and profile are removed immediately when you confirm.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email rohitgupta12371380@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account, with the subject line "Delete my account". We will verify your identity and delete your account within 30 days.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile information (name, username, email, phone number, gender, location, profile photo).
  • Your connected social-account data and analytics stored by Influmart.
  • Your messages and collaboration requests.
  • Your content-insight data.

What data may be retained

We may retain a limited record of transactions and payments (for example, subscription/invoice records processed by our payment provider, Razorpay) where required to comply with legal, tax, or accounting obligations. Such records are kept only for the period required by law and are then deleted.
